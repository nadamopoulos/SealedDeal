NOVATECH SOLUTIONS GMBH - CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION MEMORANDUM

1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
NovaTech Solutions GmbH is a leading enterprise SaaS provider headquartered in Munich, Germany, specializing in supply chain optimization and procurement intelligence software. Founded in 2015 by Dr. Thomas Weber (CEO) and Maria Schneider (CTO), the company has grown to 285 employees across offices in Munich, Berlin, Vienna, and Zurich.

The company's flagship product, "NovaSuite", is a cloud-native platform that leverages AI/ML to provide real-time supply chain visibility, predictive analytics, and automated procurement workflows for mid-market and enterprise clients.

5. MARKET & COMPETITION
The global supply chain management software market was valued at EUR 19.3B in 2025 and is projected to reach EUR 37.8B by 2030 (CAGR 14.4%). Key market drivers include:
- Post-pandemic supply chain resilience initiatives
- ESG reporting requirements increasing demand for supply chain transparency
- AI/ML adoption in procurement processes
- Growing regulatory requirements (EU Supply Chain Due Diligence Act)

Key competitors include SAP Ariba, Coupa Software, Jaggaer, and Ivalua. NovaTech differentiates through:
- Superior AI-powered demand forecasting (proprietary algorithms)
- DACH-specific compliance modules
- Faster implementation cycles (avg 8 weeks vs industry standard 16-24 weeks)
- Deep integration with German ERP systems
